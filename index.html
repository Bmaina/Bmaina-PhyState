01 — VISION

AI agents should never act on an unverified physical claim.

As AI agents take on decisions that used to require a person to look — releasing a credit tranche, paying a claim, approving a disbursement — the physical world becomes the one input they cannot independently confirm.

PhyState exists to close that gap: an independent layer that checks whether what an agent believes about a physical asset is actually true, before the decision is made.
